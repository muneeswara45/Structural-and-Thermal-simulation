# Structural-and-Thermal-simulation
Structural Analysis

A structural analysis was performed in CATIA/3DEXPERIENCE to evaluate the stress distribution in the piston under the applied loading conditions. A finite element mesh was generated for the model, and suitable material properties, interactions, and boundary conditions were applied. The Von Mises stress contour plot shows the variation of stress across the piston, with higher stress regions represented by warmer colors and lower stress regions by cooler colors. The results help identify critical areas of the piston that experience greater loading and are useful for evaluating its structural integrity.

The simulation demonstrates how the piston responds to the applied static load and helps in identifying regions that may require design improvement. The analysis can be used to optimize the piston geometry while maintaining adequate strength and reducing unnecessary material.

Thermal Analysis

A durability analysis was also performed on the crankshaft to evaluate its behavior under repeated loading conditions. The crankshaft was meshed using finite elements, and material properties and structural loading conditions were defined. The durability result displays the estimated life in repeated cycles, with the shown result indicating approximately 5 × 10⁵ repeats for the analyzed condition.

The durability simulation helps in understanding the crankshaft's ability to withstand repeated operating loads over time. It also helps identify potential fatigue-sensitive regions and provides useful information for improving the crankshaft design, material selection, and overall durability.

Simulation Inference

The structural and durability simulations provide a better understanding of the mechanical components under operating loads. The structural analysis helps evaluate stress distribution and potential critical regions, while the durability analysis helps assess fatigue life under repeated loading. Overall, these simulations improve the design validation process by providing useful information for optimizing the piston and crankshaft for strength, reliability, and service life.
